# FAQ

## Can a requestor cancel after manager approval?
No. Cancellation is allowed only while Draft or Pending Manager Approval.

## Why do I not see an approval button?
Your role or the current status does not allow that action.

## Why is rejection failing?
Rejection requires remarks.

## Can SystemAdmin approve requests?
No. SystemAdmin can view all requests and manage types, but approval authority stays with Manager and FinanceAdmin.
